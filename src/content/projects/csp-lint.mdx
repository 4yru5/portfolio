## Problem

A CSP looks strict and still permits inline script via a forgotten
`unsafe-inline` or an over-broad host. The gap is hard to see by reading.

## Approach

`csp-lint` parses the policy and reports the concrete bypasses each directive
leaves open, ranked by impact.

## Architecture

A Rust core compiled to WASM so it runs in the browser and in CI with the same
logic.

## Results

Turned "the CSP looks fine" into a specific list of what to tighten.
